# Assets da hero Zenith

Imagens criadas pela ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, com base na direção visual das referências fornecidas. As fotografias foram recriadas; os títulos, botões, cartões, mapa e molduras são elementos HTML/CSS/SVG responsivos.

## Arquivos finais

- `public/assets/zenith-drone-sunset.webp` — cena principal, 1536 × 1024.
- `public/assets/zenith-drone-sunset-800.webp` — versão menor, 800 × 533.
- `public/assets/zenith-field-sunset.webp` — fundo sem drone, 1536 × 1024.
- `public/assets/fonts/inter-latin-variable.woff2` — fonte local; licença em `public/assets/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt`.

## Prompt da cena principal

Create a photorealistic landscape website asset, 1536x1024. A dark charcoal professional camera quadcopter drone hovering close above a lush green soybean plantation at golden sunset. Drone is centered horizontally at x=51%, y=27%, occupies about 63% of frame width; seen front three quarter perspective, detailed gimbal camera, four arms, naturally motion blurred propellers, tiny green indicator lights. Horizon at 51% of image height, distant dark treeline, warm sun at far right near horizon. Beautiful blue-grey cloudy sky in top half lit by gold, textured soybean leaves in bottom half with some softly blurred foreground leaves at very bottom. Deep forest green and golden warm photographic palette. Match the drone photography scene in supplied desktop and mobile website references. Produce ONLY the clean full bleed photograph, NO website design, NO borders, NO cards, NO text, NO numbers, NO logos, NO watermarks, NO charts or overlays.

## Prompt do fundo

Edit this photograph: remove the drone entirely and naturally reconstruct the cloudy sky behind it. Keep the soybean field, sun and beautiful cloudy golden sunset. Wide landscape full-bleed background photograph for a website. Raise horizon to 38% from top and emphasize beautiful lush soybean leaves in foreground filling bottom 62%, slight soft bokeh. No drone, no people, no text, no UI or graphics. Same dark forest green and warm golden colors. 1536x1024.
